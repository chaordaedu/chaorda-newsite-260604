## Claims To Make

- AI agents can retrieve, reason, and act, but still miss human state.
- Human-agent interaction quality will become a major adoption bottleneck.
- Real-time emotional state prediction is the first high-value wedge.
- Privacy-preserving consent architecture is part of the product, not a compliance add-on.
- Human-agent interaction data can compound into a defensible platform moat.

- Chaorda gives AI true empathy.
- Chaorda understands all human emotion.
- Chaorda reads emotions without user consent.
- Chaorda replaces foundation models.

## Strategic Constraint

The website must not present Chaorda as an OpenAI-like general AI lab, an Anthropic-like safety lab, a Perplexity-like answer engine, or a Runway-like media generation company.

Chaorda is a category-defining infrastructure company for the human layer of AI agents.

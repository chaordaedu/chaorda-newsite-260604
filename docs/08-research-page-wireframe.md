## Content Rules

- Use precise technical language and avoid exaggerated claims.
- Separate demonstrated capability from roadmap speculation.
- Include privacy and consent in every research theme.
- Provide plain-language summaries alongside technical notes.

## Benchmark Alignment

- OpenAI: research index and latest advancement style can guide publication organization.
- Anthropic: safety, commitments, and responsible scaling style should guide tone.
- Perplexity: citation-first clarity should influence summaries and source references.
- Runway: visual research artifacts should make complex model behavior inspectable.

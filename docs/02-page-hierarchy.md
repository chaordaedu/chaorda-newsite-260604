# Page Hierarchy

## Global Hierarchy

Every page should follow this order:

1. Page hero with one clear thesis.
2. Proof module showing product, research, security, or market evidence.
3. Deep content sections with scannable technical hierarchy.
4. Conversion module tailored to the visitor persona.
5. Footer with full navigation, policy, and contact routes.

## Home

Primary goal: establish Chaorda as the emotional layer of artificial intelligence.

Hierarchy:

1. Hero: "The emotional layer of artificial intelligence."
2. Ambient WebGL proof surface.
3. Behavioral validation split-screen: vanilla LLM vs Chaorda adaptive runtime.
4. Three-tier architecture preview.
5. SDK preview with TypeScript/Python tabs.
6. Data flywheel and defensibility preview.
7. Consent and privacy proof.
8. Persona CTA row.

## Responsive Behavior

- Desktop: hero copy and ambient visual share the first viewport.
- Tablet: hero stacks with canvas below copy.
- Mobile: canvas becomes compact signal field; all diagrams become vertical timelines.
- CTAs remain visible without covering hero content.

## Content Priorities

1. Position Chaorda as infrastructure.
2. Demonstrate emotional prediction visually.
3. Prove technical architecture.
4. Show developer usability.
5. Establish privacy seriousness.
6. Route each persona.

## Benchmark Alignment

- OpenAI: simple hero, clear CTAs, editorial/news-like section density.
- Anthropic: responsible framing and research credibility appear above the fold or immediately after.
- Perplexity: direct product proof and enterprise utility are visible before conversion.
- Runway: immersive first-screen visual proof supports the category claim.

## Goal

Give institutional investors enough clarity to understand the category, defensibility, and privacy advantage before requesting private data-room access.

## Conversion Rules

- Do not expose confidential metrics publicly.
- Make the data-room request feel selective and institutional.
- Route qualified requests to CRM and Slack for manual approval.
- Show privacy architecture before the form to reduce diligence friction.

## Benchmark Alignment

- OpenAI: institutional tone and sparse, confident claims.
- Anthropic: trust, safety, and responsible deployment integrated into investor story.
- Perplexity: enterprise proof, security, and FAQ before conversion.
- Runway: big category framing and strong visual moat diagram.

## Sitemap Rationale

The PRD defines three funnels: institutional investors, enterprise product architects, and applied ML research engineers. The sitemap keeps each funnel one click from the homepage while preserving a simple top-level structure comparable to OpenAI's restrained global navigation.

## Benchmark Alignment

- OpenAI: Similar clarity in primary navigation and separation of research, platform, business, and company.
- Anthropic: Research and responsible deployment remain first-class destinations.
- Perplexity: Platform and enterprise conversion flows are explicit, with FAQ and security proof.
- Runway: Home and Technology carry immediate visual proof rather than relying only on copy.

# Benchmark Comparison

## Sources Reviewed

- OpenAI: https://openai.com/
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/
- Perplexity Enterprise: https://www.perplexity.ai/enterprise
- Perplexity Hub: https://www.perplexity.ai/hub
- Runway: https://runwayml.com/

## Strategic Read

Chaorda should not copy any one reference. The approved direction should combine OpenAI's navigational restraint, Anthropic's institutional trust language, Perplexity's proof-led enterprise conversion flow, and Runway's immersive technical demonstration.

## OpenAI Comparison

OpenAI uses a concise global navigation that separates Research, Products, Business, Developers, and Company. Its homepage behaves like an institutional command center: hero prompt, recent news, stories, research, and business proof. Chaorda should borrow the simple navigation and high-density editorial layout, but avoid becoming a news feed too early. Chaorda needs stronger first-screen technical proof because its category is less familiar.

Recommendation for Chaorda:

- Use a restrained top nav with seven destinations only.
- Keep CTAs simple: "Request access", "Explore technology", "Read research".
- Use dense but calm sectioning, with clear editorial hierarchy.
- Avoid over-explaining the product in marketing prose.

## Anthropic Comparison

Anthropic foregrounds safety, commitments, research, trust, and company purpose. Its structure creates confidence through long-term responsibility and academic gravity. Chaorda's emotion-prediction domain is sensitive, so the privacy and consent story must be part of the architecture, not a legal afterthought.

Recommendation for Chaorda:

- Put Research and Privacy in the primary site journey.
- Use serif accent typography for manifesto, research, and consent language.
- Expose ethics, zero-knowledge processing, data retention, and consent mechanics in product pages.
- Make claims falsifiable and technical, not sentimental.

## Perplexity Comparison

Perplexity Enterprise organizes around direct utility: accurate AI for teams, model orchestration, internal knowledge, external research, browser workflows, enterprise security, use cases, proof metrics, customer stories, FAQ, and conversion. Chaorda should borrow this buyer-friendly clarity for Platform and Investors pages.

Recommendation for Chaorda:

- Make "what it does" visible in the first two sections of every page.
- Include enterprise security and privacy proof before conversion forms.
- Use FAQ sections on Platform, Investors, and Contact pages.
- Design investor qualification as a credible workflow rather than a generic contact form.

## Runway Comparison

Runway relies on immediate visual proof and an ambitious category statement. It uses immersive media, industry pathways, and research/product/company links to position itself as frontier infrastructure. Chaorda needs this level of visual conviction for multimodal emotional perception.

Recommendation for Chaorda:

- Use a live ambient WebGL/Three.js hero that suggests emotional signal fields.
- Pair visual motion with concise technical labels so the effect does not feel decorative.
- Include industry pathways: customer experience, digital health, adaptive coaching, entertainment companions.
- Keep visual assets performant and purposeful.

## Final Positioning Decision

Chaorda should present itself as a frontier infrastructure company with three simultaneous proofs:

- Technical proof: architecture maps, SDK examples, latency and privacy constraints.
- Ethical proof: explicit consent, edge tokenization, no raw biometric storage.
- Market proof: investor moat, data flywheel, enterprise use cases, retention outcomes.

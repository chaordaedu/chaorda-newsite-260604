# Final Positioning Recommendation

## Final Decision

Chaorda should not define itself primarily as an **Emotional Intelligence Infrastructure** company.

That phrase is useful, but it is too narrow and too easy to misunderstand for a company that wants to become a large, venture-scale platform business.

The recommended company category is:

```text
Human Understanding Infrastructure
```

The recommended first product category is:

```text
Real-time Emotional State Prediction for AI Agents
```

This structure gives Chaorda both a large category and a sharp wedge.

---

## Why This Is The Strongest Position

### Emotional Intelligence Is The Wedge, Not The Whole Company

The PRD correctly identifies the immediate problem: current AI agents are emotionally blind. They miss vocal tone, micro-expressions, pacing, text inflection, frustration, uncertainty, and preference drift.

That is a powerful wedge.

But if Chaorda owns only "emotional intelligence," the market may perceive the company as:

- A sentiment analysis API.
- A chatbot empathy layer.
- A digital health or coaching tool.
- A customer support optimization product.
- A sensitive emotion-tracking vendor.

Those perceptions reduce the apparent scale of the business.

### Human Understanding Is The Platform

"Human Understanding Infrastructure" expands the company beyond emotion while preserving the original thesis.

It allows Chaorda to own a broader infrastructure layer:

- Emotional state.
- Preference drift.
- Trust readiness.
- Frustration and uncertainty.
- Cognitive load.
- Interaction timing.
- Intent ambiguity.
- Escalation risk.
- Human-agent retention.
- Adaptive response policy.

This is a better platform category because it describes the missing layer between AI capability and human adoption.
